Lifetime taxes paid vs. after-tax estate value

Each blue dot is one candidate conversion schedule the search tried. Hover to inspect a point. Hollow markers trace the optimized/current-plan/no-conversions schedules through age 87, 90, 93 -- hover a marker for its exact numbers.

After-tax estate value = taxable + Roth + inherited-Roth balances, plus Traditional (owned + inherited) balances taxed at the 24% assumed heir rate -- valued as of the household's own stated life expectancy, not necessarily the projection's last row. Lifetime taxes paid sums the household's own federal tax owed every year from the start of the projection through that same horizon.

The search is coordinate-ascent / pattern search (nudge one year's amount at a time from a few starting points, keep whatever improves the objective), not a random Monte Carlo sweep -- so this cloud traces the paths the search explored on its way to a good schedule, rather than a uniform random sample of the whole space.
