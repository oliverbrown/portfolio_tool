# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to
  investing and retirement planning.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project's users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information -- including anyone's account
  numbers, balances, or other financial details -- without their explicit
  permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in
  a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces -- issues, pull requests, discussions,
and commit messages -- and when someone is representing the project
elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that don't follow this code of conduct,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

To report a problem privately, use the repository's
[**Report a vulnerability**](../../security/advisories/new) form (it
reaches only the maintainer) and say it's a code-of-conduct report. If
that isn't possible, open an issue asking for a private contact -- without
the details -- and the maintainer will follow up. All reports are
reviewed and handled confidentially.
